Your photographs

Photos are swapped as files, which is simpler and safer than uploading them through a form.

  1. Find the photo you want to change in the list below and note its file name.
  2. Rename your own photo to exactly that name, including the .jpg ending.
  3. Copy it into the matching folder, replacing the placeholder.
  4. Refresh the website. Your photo is live.
Aim for about 1600 pixels wide. Larger than that only makes the site slower to load on a phone.
This editor runs entirely inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
