Your photographs

Photos are still swapped as files, which is simpler and safer than uploading them through a form — only the fixed photo slots below (hero images, the dishes above, the gallery) exist to point at.

  1. Find the photo you want to change in the list below and note its file name.
  2. Rename your own photo to exactly that name, including the .jpg ending.
  3. Copy it into the matching folder in your website files, replacing the placeholder.
  4. Refresh the website. Your photo is live within a minute or so.
Aim for about 1600 pixels wide. Larger than that only makes the site slower to load on a phone.
